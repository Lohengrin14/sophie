# Запросы генерации

Способ: встроенный imagegen, два отдельных запроса. Изображения — дизайн-концепты, не скриншоты приложения. Код завершён и закоммичен до генерации. Ниже точные запросы, отправленные инструменту.

## 1 · Paper / Cobalt

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: high-fidelity website art-direction presentation, landscape 1536 × 1024.
Create a polished design presentation for an independent painter's portfolio and original painting inquiry website. Fresh editorial gallery, very little copy, large paintings and a one-line footer. Show a desktop homepage occupying left 75% and a slim mobile homepage at right, both front-on with no physical device frame. Bottom margin five palette swatches with readable hex labels and the direction title.
Desktop: compact header “ATELIER.”, navigation “Works / Commissions / About / Contact”, language “RU / EN”. Huge refined modern sans-serif heading “Art, a little closer.” with a small accent-coloured asterisk. One short line “Original paintings. For your everyday.” and understated “Explore works ↗”. Below, asymmetric editorial exhibition: large olive-grove oil painting on a pale gallery field; narrow right column “Colour. Light. Something personal.” and a smaller quiet sea painting. Then two image-led collection cards, small titles and euro prices, and compact accent-coloured commission band “What moves you? ↗”. Single-line footer “© ATELIER. / Instagram / Privacy”. Mobile stacks same content with small menu icon.
Realistic original-looking impasto landscape demo paintings, no photographs of people. Paintings keep natural olive, gold, sea-blue hues. Precise grid, modern elegant sans-serif, generous art-focused spacing, thin rules. No generic marketing cards, gradients, drop shadows, fake client logos, testimonials. Short correctly spelled text. Small “DESIGN CONCEPT” outside website. Feel like an art publication with welcoming functional inquiry path, not SaaS.
Direction title “01 / PAPER + COBALT”. Palette paper #F5F5F2, ink #1E2026, cobalt #293EDB, pale lavender gallery field #E5E5ED, white #FFFFFF. Predominantly light, decisive small cobalt areas.
```
